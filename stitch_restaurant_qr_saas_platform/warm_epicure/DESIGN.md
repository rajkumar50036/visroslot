## Brand & Style

This design system defines a premium, contactless dining companion tailored specifically for in-restaurant QR table ordering. It eliminates friction while honoring the sensory warmth of hospitality.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Sensory & Appetizing:** Rich culinary cues through warm tones, generous imagery containers, and deliberate micro-interactions that make dishes feel tactile and desirable.
- **Effortless & Unobtrusive:** The digital interface defers to the physical dining experience; ordering must feel seamless, frictionless, and swift without technical overhead.
- **Hospitality-Grade Elegance:** Balances the casual utility of quick-ordering with the polish of high-end bistro collateral—structured, spacious, and grounded.

### Design Movement: Modern Tactile Minimalism
The system pairs contemporary structural clarity with warm, organic tactile nuance:
- Clean, open surfaces built on creamy porcelain and parchment neutrals rather than sterile digital grays.
- Expressive food imagery complemented by delicate inner borders and warm ambient glow rather than harsh drop shadows.
- Micro-elevations and fluid bottom-sheet sheets anchored firmly for natural one-handed thumb-zone operation.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, culinary foundation. Warm whites and parchment tones eliminate blue-light clinical glare, while deep saffron and terracotta spark hunger and drive deliberate focal points.

### Palette Architecture
- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#fcfbf9` (Warm Cream)
  - Elevated surfaces & card fills: `#ffffff` (Clean Porcelain)
  - Recessed backgrounds & subtle chip fills: `#f7f4ee` (Parchment Tint)
  - Subtle structural dividers: `#e7e2d7` (Warm Stroke)
- **Primary & Interactive Accents:**
  - Primary Action / Saffron: `#ea580c` (Vibrant appetizing saffron; key CTAs, stepper selections, cart badge)
  - Primary Hover / Terracotta: `#c2410c` (Pressed states, emphasized pricing accents)
  - Surface Orange Glow: `rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)` (Active state fills, selection rings)
- **Typography & Dark Neutrals:**
  - Ink Primary: `#1c1917` (Deep stone-black charcoal; headers, item titles, prices)
  - Ink Secondary: `#57534e` (Warm charcoal medium; descriptions, modifier notes)
  - Ink Tertiary: `#a8a29e` (Muted caption, empty state hints)
- **Dietary & Service Signals:**
  - Vegetarian Indicator: `#16a34a` (Crisp leaf green; subtle icon badge)
  - Non-Vegetarian / Alert: `#dc2626` (Crimson indicator badge)
  - Pay at Counter Badge Fill: `#fef3c7` (Soft warm amber tone)
  - Pay at Counter Badge Text: `#92400e` (Grounded deep amber text)

## Typography

Typography prioritizes legibility in ambient, low-light dining atmospheres while retaining modern culinary elegance.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Behavior
- **Headings & Titles:** Set in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern editorial weight on food item headers and category titles.
- **Body & Menu Descriptions:** Rendered in `body-md` with balanced line-heights (`1.35rem`) to ensure appetizing culinary ingredient lists read effortlessly on small hand-held viewports without cluttering the screen.
- **Currency & Pricing:** Formatted with `price-display` in tabular/proportional figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so numbers align vertically across custom lists and cart summaries.
- **Service Tags & Dietary Badges:** Upper-case or compact heavy tracking (`label-sm`, letter spacing `0.04em`) reserved strictly for status pills, dietary badges, and table indicators.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly mobile-first architecture engineered for one-handed thumb reach during dining, with graceful scaling for tablets and counter displays.

### Layout Model
- **Mobile Grid (Primary < 768px):** Single column fluid canvas bounded by `1rem` outer canvas margins. Elements stretch horizontally across comfortable 16px lateral padding. Sticky controls occupy bottom safe areas.
- **Tablet / Large Screen (≥ 768px):** A constrained central layout container (`max-width: 640px` or split-view `max-width: 1024px`) to preserve intimate menu ergonomics and avoid expansive eye travel. Two-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters for menu item cards.

### Spacing Principles
- **Touch Targets:** Minimum interactive target is strictly `44px × 44px` across category pills, dietary filters, and stepper increments.
- **Safe Area Insets:** All sticky components (floating cart, bottom sheets) incorporate native browser `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` alongside `space-md` clearance to avoid navigation bar collision.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy employs warm ambient light diffusion and layered surface tints rather than harsh drop shadows, preserving an authentic tactile dining aesthetic.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#fcfbf9` cream background. Zero shadow.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Food Cards & Chips):** Soft structural isolation.
  - Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.03)`
  - Border: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(231, 226, 215, 0.7)`
- **Tier 2 (Active States, Floating Buttons & Sticky Top Nav):**
  - Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -1px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.04)`
  - Border: 1px continuous hairline `rgba(231, 226, 215, 0.9)`
- **Tier 3 (Floating Cart Bar):** Warm prominent suspension.
  - Shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.16), 0 4px 12px rgba(234, 88, 12, 0.08)`
- **Tier 4 (Bottom Sheets & Modals):**
  - Backdrop: `rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.5)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`
  - Sheet Shadow: `0 -8px 32px rgba(28, 25, 23, 0.12)`

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary employs organic curvature, reinforcing hospitality and culinary comfort.

### Corner Curvature Scale
- **Base Level (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Small elements, dietary icon containers, text inputs, and table number indicators.
- **Large Level (`1rem` / `16px`):** Food item cards, modifier selector panels, and cart summary containers.
- **Extra Large Level (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Sheet tops, promotional hero banners, and floating cart capsule wrappers.
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Category navigation chips, Stepper `- / +` controllers, primary CTA buttons, and status indicator tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Order Button:** Saffron orange (`#ea580c`) fill, white bold typography (`label-lg`), pill shape (`9999px`), `48px` default height. Pressed state deepens to terracotta (`#c2410c`) with subtle scale `scale(0.98)`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#e7e2d7`, ink `#1c1917`.
- **Quantity Stepper (`- / +`):** Oval/pill capsule, `#f7f4ee` background, `#1c1917` icons. Active quantity rendered in bold tabular digits.

### Category Chips
- **Resting:** Low-contrast background `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #e7e2d7`, text `#57534e`, fully rounded pill.
- **Selected:** High-contrast background `#1c1917`, text `#ffffff`, border `1px solid #1c1917`, accompanied by subtle scale accent. Horizontally scrollable with zero scrollbar visible.

### Food Item Cards
- **Compact List Variant:** Horizontal layout, clean white card surface with warm hairline border, `16px` internal padding. Title in `title-md`, price in `price-display`. Thumbnail `88px × 88px` rounded at `12px` on the right side with an overlapping absolute "+ Add" pill button.
- **Hero / Featured Variant:** Vertical layout with full-width aspect-ratio `16:9` food photography, curved at `16px`, with dietary indicator badge floated top-left.

### Dietary Badges
- **Vegetarian Badge:** Square outline `14px × 14px` in `#16a34a` with rounded corners `3px`, containing a centered filled solid circle `6px × 6px` in `#16a34a`.
- **Non-Vegetarian Badge:** Triangle outline in `#dc2626` with matching centered red dot.

### Bottom Sheet Modals
- Slides up from the bottom with a spring curve (`cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)`).
- Features a top drag handle (`36px × 4px` rounded bar, `#d6d1c7`) centered `8px` from the sheet top.
- Includes sticky footer container housing modifier price calculations and confirmation button.

### Floating Sticky Cart
- Fixed bar anchored `16px` above the viewport bottom (respecting device safe margins).
- Full capsule pill design (`rounded-full`) with `#1c1917` dark background and white typography, or vibrant saffron `#ea580c`.
- Displays item count badge on the left, total amount centered, and action prompt ("View Cart →") on the right.

### Table & Service Badges
- **Pay at Counter Badge:** High-visibility pill badge with soft amber fill (`#fef3c7`), dark amber text (`#92400e`), and tiny receipt/counter glyph.
- **Table Marker:** Crisp header indicator showing e.g., "Table 12" wrapped in a parchment pill with a subtle pulse dot indicating active dine-in session.

# 8. ₹2 AUTOMATIC REFUND – AUTOPAY VERIFICATION ONLY

This is a critical business requirement.

The ₹2 refund workflow must apply exclusively to an actual temporary verification deduction associated with Razorpay AutoPay authorization.

Rules:

1. If a ₹2 verification deduction actually occurs, identify and record that transaction.
2. Let Razorpay handle its automatic authorization refund wherever supported.
3. Verify the refund through Razorpay's backend APIs and webhook events.
4. Display the actual refund status to the customer.
5. Prevent duplicate refunds.
6. If no verification deduction occurs, do not create a refund.
7. If Razorpay uses another authorization amount for a supported payment method, follow its actual documented process.
8. Do not independently initiate another refund if Razorpay has already initiated an automatic refund.
9. Do not mark a refund successful without provider confirmation.
10. Handle pending, failed and delayed refund scenarios.

**STRICTLY EXCLUDED FROM THIS REFUND FEATURE:**

- Professional monthly subscription payments.
- Professional yearly subscription payments.
- Business monthly subscription payments.
- Business yearly subscription payments.
- Any actual recurring subscription charge.

Never automatically refund a subscription payment through this verification-refund workflow.

Do not create a separate ₹2 transaction for every customer.

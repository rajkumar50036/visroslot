Frequently Asked Questions

Essential details regarding trial terms, multi-practitioner scaling, and channel distribution.

What is VISROSLOT and who is it designed for?

expand_more

VISROSLOT is a dedicated appointment booking and customer scheduling platform built for service businesses like salons, dental clinics, spas, fitness studios, and independent consultants. It replaces phone-call coordination with an automated booking link, staff scheduling calendar, and customer directory.

How does the 7-day free trial operate?

expand_more

Eligible businesses receive 7 days of full platform access with live scheduling tools, customer booking links, and dashboard analytics. This lets you test customer bookings, configure staff services, and review the dashboard completely risk-free with zero upfront commitments.

Can I use my booking link directly inside WhatsApp and Instagram?

expand_more

Yes. Every practice receives a short, elegant dedicated URL (e.g., visroslot.live/aura) and a high-resolution QR plaque. You can embed this link directly in your Instagram bio, WhatsApp auto-replies, Google Business Profile, or physical storefront banners.

How are client records and personal data secured?

expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using AES 256-bit cryptography. Unlike consumer marketplaces, VISROSLOT does not poach or market alternative businesses to your clientele. You retain 100% sovereign ownership of your client lists and service histories.

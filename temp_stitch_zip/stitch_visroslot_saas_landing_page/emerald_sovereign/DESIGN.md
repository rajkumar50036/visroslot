## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, high-prestige enterprise aesthetic tailored for high-stakes digital infrastructure, bespoke scheduling architecture, and executive fintech operations. Synthesizing the quiet restraint of Swiss typography, the structural discipline of modern developer platforms like Linear and Stripe, and the tactility of private wealth management, the UI communicates uncompromising institutional reliability and technical refinement.

The visual direction centers on **Editorial Enterprise Minimalism with Architectural Precision**. Rather than generic flat screens or consumer-grade gradients, the system utilizes deeply pigmented forest greens, crystalline white surfaces, champagne hairline borders, and warm ivory editorial breathing space. Contrast is deliberate and asymmetric: commanding dark-mode hero canvases and conversion blocks anchor the experience, transitioning into luminous, surgical light-mode console views for data matrices and interactive workflows.

All interactions are characterized by mathematical alignment, low-key elevation transitions, micro-radii, and precise tabular typography. Decorative clutter, gratuitous roundedness, and ungrounded novelty are firmly excluded in favor of timeless executive craft.

## Colors

The color system is organized around a dual-tonal balance: deep, commanding forest tones for structural authority and high-contrast anchors, paired with airy, warm neutrals and crisp whites for interactive consoles and editorial legibility.

### Hierarchy & Functional Roles

- **Deep Emerald (`#043D2E`)**: The primary brand anchor. Governs dominant headers, hero containers, primary navigation masts, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Royal Emerald (`#046A38`)**: The primary interactive engine. Applied to primary call-to-action buttons, selected tab indicators, active navigation states, verified indicators, and high-priority metrics.
- **Forest Black (`#10251D`)**: The ultimate tertiary depth color. Utilized for terminal conversion blocks, deep ground footers, persistent status banners, and key high-contrast CTA zones.
- **Warm Ivory (`#F7F6F0`)**: The primary neutral background. Offers an editorial, low-glare canvas for long-form case narratives, comparative matrices, and alternating section foundations.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)**: The core interactive surface. Reserved for elevated workspace cards, real-time schedule grids, data-dense tables, dialog panels, and console frames.
- **Champagne Silver (`#D8DED8`)**: The structural divider. Serves strictly as a 1px boundary line, border outline, table grid rule, and timeline demarcator to construct spatial order without heavy visual weight.
- **Soft Sage (`#A7B9AA`)**: The auxiliary indicator. Deployed for subtle secondary tags, micro-badges, inactive metadata readouts, and soft contextual buffer tags.

### Strict Palette Governance
- Saturated warm hues (orange, red, yellow, purple, lavender, pink, neon lime) are strictly prohibited.
- Status, verification, and critical updates are communicated exclusively through emerald tints, soft sage fills, champagne borders, and explicit iconography.

## Typography

Typography pairs **Manrope** for architectural headlines with **Inter** for operational consoles, data-dense tables, and functional micro-copy.

- **Manrope** provides sculpted proportions with tightened letter-spacing at display scales, lending editorial weight and modern industrial confidence to page headers, pricing callouts, and section intros.
- **Inter** handles high-volume reading, form labels, and table cells. It provides neutrality, crisp legibility at small point sizes, and robust vertical metric balance.
- **Tabular Numerals**: All monetary sums, time values, slot coordinates, and data metric indicators must enforce `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee vertical column alignment across dashboards.
- **Tracking Discipline**: Display levels enforce negative letter spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to eliminate typographic float. Micro labels and technical badges leverage subtle positive tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to preserve legibility when rendered in uppercase.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8px rhythmic foundation, emphasizing asymmetric architectural balance and generous structural breathing room.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Architecture
- **Desktop (1280px and above)**: 12-column responsive grid with `gutter-lg` (`2.5rem`) and outer margins of `margin-lg` (`4rem`). Console workflows utilize a fixed 280px left rail alongside a flexible operational canvas. Editorial showcases feature asymmetric column distribution (e.g., 5-column editorial context paired with 7-column interactive slot consoles).
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column responsive grid with `gutter` (`1.5rem`) and `margin` (`2rem`). Lateral toolbars collapse into a unified header bar or slide-over drawer to protect tabular workspace width.
- **Mobile (Below 768px)**: 4-column layout with `gutter-sm` (`1rem`) and `margin-sm` (`1rem`). Side-by-side matrices collapse into stacked, sequential card stacks with horizontal pill filters.

### Internal Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro gaps between icons and inline status text.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal padding for tags, time slot pills, and dense table cell padding.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard form inputs, action button horizontal padding, and card micro-gaps.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard card interior padding, flyout panel margins, and metric module gutters.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section breaks within consoles and structural spacing between disparate metric cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **structural hairline boundaries and subtle tonal separation**, avoiding heavy, synthetic drop shadows.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas)**: `#F7F6F0` (Warm Ivory) for editorial narratives or `#FFFFFF` (Pure White) for transactional console backgrounds. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Console Frames)**: `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a 1px hairline border of `#D8DED8` (Champagne Silver). Shadows are absent, prioritizing architectural crispness.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Dropdowns)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border of `#D8DED8` and an ambient, emerald-tinted shadow: `0 4px 20px -4px rgba(4, 61, 46, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(16, 37, 29, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Flyout Sheets)**: `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border of `#D8DED8` paired with an executive ambient shadow: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(4, 61, 46, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -2px rgba(16, 37, 29, 0.06)`. Backdrops use `#10251D` (Forest Black) at 45% opacity with an optional 4px blur.
- **Inverted Level (Hero & Conversion Modules)**: `#043D2E` or `#10251D` deep grounds with interior white or sage typography and subtle 1px borders tinted in `rgba(216, 222, 216, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a soft, controlled radius standard (`roundedness: 1`), yielding an executive, tailored finish.

- **Primary UI Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Cards, Dropdowns)**: Set to `0.25rem` (4px) to retain an architectural, enterprise-grade discipline.
- **Containers & Modal Shells (`rounded-lg`)**: Set to `0.5rem` (8px) for structural frames and dialogs.
- **Major Showcase Panes (`rounded-xl`)**: Set to `0.75rem` (12px) exclusively for expansive mockups and console framing viewports.
- **Status Pills & Micro Chips**: Use `9999px` (fully rounded) to establish intentional functional contrast against clean rectangular containers.

## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an elevated, institutional grade of software aesthetics—blending the rigorous precision of modern enterprise SaaS with the timeless restraint of private banking and prestige architectural finishes. The emotional goal is immediate trust, quiet confidence, and clarity under high-stakes workflows.

The visual style unites **Corporate / Modern** structure with **Tonal Luxury Minimalist** accents:
- **Structural Integrity:** Crisp grid lines, deliberate proportions, and strictly controlled visual density.
- **Restraint Over Spectacle:** Deep jewel tones and subtle platinum delineation eliminate the need for gratuitous embellishments or loud gradients.
- **Editorial Finishes:** Generous white space punctuated by deep forest containers and jewel emerald interactions.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by natural deep greens and crisp silvery neutrals, purposefully excluding warm saturated tones, purples, lavenders, and neon flourishes.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary Accent (`#046A38`):** Emerald Green. Reserved for focal interaction points, active indicators, and high-priority actions.
- **Supporting / Container (`#123D2B`):** Deep Forest. Applied to authoritative structural sections, primary navigation bars, footers, and executive summaries.
- **Base Canvas (`#F8FAF9`):** Pearl White. The foundational surface background providing high legibility and breathability.
- **Structural Lines (`#DCE5E0`):** Platinum Silver. Used for hair-thin dividers, borders, and crisp card strokes.
- **Primary Content (`#172B25`):** Dark Spruce Text. The primary reading color for headings, titles, and critical data figures.
- **Secondary Content (`#4D645B`):** Muted Forest Slate. Used for metadata, captions, input hints, and secondary labels.

### Surface System
- `surface-canvas`: `#F8FAF9` (Main backdrop)
- `surface-elevated`: `#FFFFFF` (Floating cards and dropdown menus)
- `surface-muted`: `#EFF3F1` (Subtle inset panels and table header tracks)
- `surface-inverted`: `#123D2B` (High-contrast hero cards, executive headers, and footers)
- `on-inverted`: `#F8FAF9` with secondary text muted to `#A3B8B0`

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances executive geometric authority with systematic legibility.

- **Headings & Display (`Manrope`):** Delivers clean geometry, crisp apexes, and modern proportion. Tight tracking is enforced at larger scale to cultivate a tailored, architectural quality.
- **Body & Controls (`Inter`):** Chosen for optical neutrality, robust numeric alignment, and unmatched legibility in analytical SaaS tables, inputs, and dense UI hierarchies.
- **Numeric Figures:** Always enable tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) within data tables, dashboards, and KPI metrics.

## Layout & Spacing

The system runs on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column responsive layout engine configured for complex SaaS dashboards and wide enterprise viewports.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout, max content boundary of `1360px`, `3rem` section margins, `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` section margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Multi-column cards collapse to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` section margins, `1rem` gutters. Tables shift to card lists or horizontal scroll containers.

### Spacing Philosophy
Internal component padding uses structured increments (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`), while section breaks rely on `2.5rem` to `4rem` to evoke an uncluttered, high-end editorial feel without sacrificing data density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses **tonal layering and low-contrast outlines** rather than heavy drop shadows.

- **Zero-Shadow Baseline:** The default page and secondary cards rest flat, differentiated solely by the `#DCE5E0` hairline stroke against `#F8FAF9`.
- **Micro Ambient Shadow:** Floating dropdowns, tooltips, and elevated modals use a featherlight shadow tinted with forest tones:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(18, 61, 43, 0.06), 0 1px 3px rgba(18, 61, 43, 0.04);`
- **Tonal Contrast:** Depth is primarily established by juxtaposing `#F8FAF9` base surfaces against crisp `#FFFFFF` card layers, anchored by full-bleed `#123D2B` deep panels.
- **Borders as Structure:** Standard border width is strictly `1px solid #DCE5E0`.

## Shapes

The geometric framework is unified around an exact **8px (0.5rem)** core radius:

- **Interactive Controls & Inputs:** Exact `8px` (`0.5rem`).
- **Cards, Modals, Panels:** Standard `8px` (`0.5rem`) for compact components; `12px` to `16px` allowed strictly for overarching full-window layout boundaries.
- **Tags & Status Badges:** Exact `8px` or tight capsule rounding where semantic distinction is required.
- **No Sharp Angles, No Oversized Bubbles:** Extreme pill buttons and zero-radius razor edges are avoided to maintain the disciplined luxury profile.

- **Inverted Primary (For Deep Forest Containers):** Background `#F8FAF9`, text `#123D2B`, hover: background `#FFFFFF`, text `#046A38`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height `42px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DCE5E0`, border-radius `8px`, text `#172B25`, placeholder `#4D645B`. Focus state: border `1px solid #046A38`, focus ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(4, 106, 56, 0.12)`.

- **Standard Card:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #DCE5E0`, border-radius `8px`, padding `1.5rem`.
- **Executive Container (Footer / Premium Section):** Background `#123D2B`, border `none`, text `#F8FAF9`, border-radius `8px` or full-bleed flush edges. Secondary elements inside use border `1px solid rgba(220, 229, 224, 0.15)`.

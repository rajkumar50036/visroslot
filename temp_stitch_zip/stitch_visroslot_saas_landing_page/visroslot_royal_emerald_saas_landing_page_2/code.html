What is VISROSLOT? expand_more

VISROSLOT is a dedicated appointment booking and customer scheduling platform built for service businesses like salons, dental clinics, spas, fitness studios, and independent consultants. It replaces phone-call coordination with an automated booking link, staff scheduling calendar, and customer directory.

How does the 7-day free trial operate? expand_more

Eligible businesses receive 7 full days of platform access. This allows you to test client bookings, configure service durations, invite specialists, and verify SMS alerts in real time with zero upfront payment obligations.

Can I use my public booking link on WhatsApp or Instagram? expand_more

Yes. Your assigned tenant URL operates across any browser without requiring an application download. You can paste it into your Instagram bio, WhatsApp catalog button, or Google Maps appointment button.

Are customer records and appointments stored securely? expand_more

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using bank-grade cryptographic protocols. We maintain a zero-monetization policy regarding your proprietary client logs and booking records.

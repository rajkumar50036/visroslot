Help & Documentation

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about VISROSLOT and our platform operations.

What is VISROSLOT? expand_more

VISROSLOT is a dedicated appointment booking and customer scheduling platform built for service businesses like salons, dental clinics, spas, fitness studios, and independent consultants. It replaces phone-call coordination with an automated booking link, staff scheduling calendar, and sovereign customer directory.

How does the 7-day free trial operate? expand_more

Eligible businesses receive 7 full days of platform access with no upfront credit card required. You can test live client bookings, configure staff rosters, and trial the interactive operational dashboard completely risk-free.

Can I use my booking link on WhatsApp & Instagram? expand_more

Yes. Every registered business receives a branded public URL and instant QR code. You can paste the URL into your Instagram bio, attach it to WhatsApp automated business replies, or place it on Google Business profiles.

Are customer records and appointments secure? expand_more

All data is strictly encrypted in transit and at rest. You retain complete, exclusive ownership of your customer database, client histories, and private formula notes without third-party data monetization.

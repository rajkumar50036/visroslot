Everything You Need to Know

Clear answers about the VISROSLOT trial, booking mechanisms, and business security.

VISROSLOT is a dedicated appointment booking and customer scheduling platform built for service businesses like salons, barbershops, dental clinics, spas, fitness studios, and independent consultants. It replaces tedious phone-call coordination with an automated public booking link, conflict-free staff calendar, and client directory.
Eligible businesses receive 7 full days of unrestricted platform access. You can configure services, add team members, test your live booking link, and experience live audio alerts. No payment is charged during the trial period, and you can cancel anytime with one click.
Yes! Your custom VISROSLOT booking link is fully responsive and optimized for mobile devices. You can paste it into your Instagram bio, attach it to your WhatsApp Business automated greetings, or print the provided QR code for your reception counter.
When kept open in any desktop or tablet browser, VISROSLOT plays a crisp audible chime and triggers a desktop push alert the moment a customer reserves a slot. The system automatically locks the time window, preventing any overlapping bookings for that specialist.

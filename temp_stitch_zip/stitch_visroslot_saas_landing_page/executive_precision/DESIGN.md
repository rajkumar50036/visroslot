## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-performance, enterprise-grade aesthetic tailored for modern appointment booking, scheduling infrastructure, and business automation. It synthesizes the visual density and crisp execution of Linear, the trusted clarity of Stripe, and the effortless scheduling accessibility of Calendly.

The brand targets founders, agency leaders, enterprise service providers, and operational teams who view appointment workflows as critical revenue infrastructure. The emotional response is grounded in absolute operational competence, frictionless clarity, and modern luxury: sharp, controlled typography, subtle border definitions, high-precision calendar matrices, and tactical kinetic depth.

The overall style is **Corporate / Modern with High-Precision SaaS Minimalism**:
- Pristine off-white foundation surfaces framed by deep midnight navy tones.
- High-contrast, hyper-refined micro-borders providing immediate visual partitioning.
- Energetic operational conversion points powered by a vivid burnt orange and subdued heritage gold trim drawn from brand identity assets.
- Subtle inner glows and crisp hairline shadows replacing heavy ambient blurring.

## Colors

The palette establishes an authoritative maritime navy core counterbalanced by warm, focused kinetic accents. The primary `#142B50` and secondary `#203F68` navy hues anchor the visual structure across navigation bars, display headings, primary CTAs, and enterprise feature containers.

Conversion triggers and real-time scheduling states leverage `#F28C28` (Vibrant Orange), establishing high visual contrast against light-gray backgrounds and deep navy backdrops. Brand heritage gold (`#C49A46`) is deployed sparingly for premium badges, VIP membership indicators, verification marks, and logo-accented micro-borders.

Surfaces default to clean white (`#FFFFFF`) layered on a gentle canvas base (`#F6F8FB`). Structural boundaries utilize hairline borders (`#E7EBF1`), ensuring crisp grid separation without visual clutter. Text hierarchy is strictly maintained with deep obsidian-navy (`#182438`) for headlines and high-clarity slate (`#687386`) for secondary details.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines **Manrope** for authoritative structural headers, **Inter** for readable informational density, and **JetBrains Mono** for developer-grade operational slot metrics, time allocations, and telemetry tags.

- **Headlines (Manrope):** Geometric precision with tightly tracked character pairs (`-0.03em` to `-0.01em`). Employs semi-bold (600) to extra-bold (800) weights to produce a decisive, modern corporate presence.
- **Body Text (Inter):** Neutral, human-tested legibility tuned for complex multi-tier scheduling cards, confirmation modals, and product pricing matrices.
- **Labels & Micro-Data (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical indicators: time slots (`09:30 AM - 10:15 AM`), booking IDs, capacity counters, and status badges.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses an **8px base grid** aligned to a **12-column responsive layout** with a maximum content container width of `1280px`.

- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) page margin. Interactive calendar grids adapt to 5 or 7 equal-column distribution.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px canvas margins. Multi-column scheduler views condense into segmented tabbed panels.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 16px canvas margins. Booking slots stack vertically with full-width action drawers.

Spacing rhythm favors breathing room within marketing heroes (`space-xl` to `80px`) while maintaining high information density within calendar slots and analytics dashboards (`space-xs` to `space-sm`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **crisp low-contrast outlines coupled with multi-layered micro-shadows**, mirroring precision software tools. Heavy, murky dropshadows are prohibited.

1. **Base Flat Surface (Level 0):** Canvas `#F6F8FB`.
2. **Elevated Card (Level 1):** Solid `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E7EBF1`) and a dual-layer hairline shadow:
   `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(16, 26, 43, 0.04), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(16, 26, 43, 0.02)`.
3. **Interactive / Hover Surface (Level 2):** Elevated interactive elements shift upward 1px, retaining border `#E7EBF1` while applying:
   `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(20, 43, 80, 0.06), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(20, 43, 80, 0.04)`.
4. **Active Booking Slots & Popovers (Level 3):** Dropdown menus, time-slot selection flyouts, and modals use:
   `0 12px 24px -4px rgba(20, 43, 80, 0.08), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(20, 43, 80, 0.04)`.
5. **Brand Accents:** Active orange booking states receive a focused colored halo:
   `0 0 0 3px rgba(242, 140, 40, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates **Roundedness Level 2 (Rounded)**:
- Core UI elements (buttons, inputs, calendar slot chips) feature standard `8px` (`0.5rem`) border radii.
- Surface cards, interactive previews, and modal windows scale up to `16px` (`1rem`) using `rounded-lg`.
- Hero feature enclosures and multi-tier pricing modules utilize `24px` (`1.5rem`) via `rounded-xl`.
- Micro status tags, circular avatar badges, and live indicator dots use full circular geometry (`9999px`).

This balanced curvature balances human approachability with structural discipline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Navy (`#142B50`) with white text, 8px radius, height 44px (10px 20px padding), font weight 600. Hover transitions to `#203F68` with subtle `-translate-y-px`.
- **Conversion CTA:** Vibrant Orange (`#F28C28`) with white text for main conversion triggers ("Start Free Trial", "Book Slot Now"). Hover transitions to `#D97706`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#E7EBF1`, text `#182438`. Hover state fills with `#F6F8FB`.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#687386`, hover text `#142B50` with `#F6F8FB` tint.

### Slot Selector Chips & Tags
- **Available Slot:** White background, 1px solid `#E7EBF1`, JetBrains Mono text (`#182438`). Hover introduces `#F28C28` border with `#FFF9F5` fill.
- **Selected Slot:** Filled `#142B50` background with white text, or filled `#F28C28` with bold indicator dot.
- **Unavailable Slot:** `#F6F8FB` fill, light border, `#9CA3AF` text with strike-through or dimmed opacity.
- **Status Badges:** JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase pills with 4px inner dot (Green `#10B981` for active, Amber `#C49A46` for enterprise, Orange `#F28C28` for pending).

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Height 44px, 8px radius, border 1px solid `#E7EBF1`, white background, 15px Inter text. Focus ring: `2px solid #142B50` with zero blur offset.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px geometry, border `#CBD5E1`. Checked state transitions to `#142B50` with a crisp white checkmark.

### Cards & Interactive Previews
- **Dashboard Preview Panels:** White canvas, hairline 1px border (`#E7EBF1`), 16px radius, subtle dual shadow. Headers feature faux window controls or segmented slot tabs.
- **Feature Cards:** 24px inner padding, containing monochromatic navy iconography within soft background badges, followed by Manrope 20px bold headings and Inter 15px secondary descriptions.
